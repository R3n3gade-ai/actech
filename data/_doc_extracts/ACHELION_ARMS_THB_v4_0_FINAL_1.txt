3. GAP 2 — VOLATILITY-ADJUSTED RE-ENTRY SIZING (VARES) 
Re-entry tranche size is inversely proportional to current VIX relative to its 90-day moving average. Higher volatility at re entry = smaller tranches. Normalized volatility = full tranches. Standard practice at every major risk-managed fund.

4.2 High-Water Mark Rules 
The HWM is the portfolio's highest ever closing NAV. Updates daily when a new high is set. NEVER decrements — only  moves up. Drawdown = (current_NAV / HWM) − 1.

5. GAP 4 — LIQUIDITY-ADJUSTED EXECUTION PROTOCOL (LAEP) 
SYS-3 execution methodology adjusts automatically based on market liquidity conditions. In normal markets, market  orders execute within 30 minutes. In elevated volatility, VWAP extends to 60 minutes. In crisis, VWAP extends to 90  minutes with priority reordering to protect the least liquid positions.

CRISIS PRIORITY REORDER RATIONALE  | In CRISIS mode (VIX >45), BSOL executes first because it is the least liquid crypto ETF — widest spread, highest  market impact. ETHB second (newer, less liquid than IBIT). IBIT third (most liquid crypto ETF). Equity last  because major AI names (NVDA, AMD) retain the deepest liquidity even in crisis. This is the OPPOSITE of normal  priority — in crisis, exit illiquid positions first while you still can.
